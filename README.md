# 🌍 Global Capital Flow Monitor
### Strategy & Capital Flow Research Initiative

**Global Capital Flow Monitor** is an independent macro-to-execution research framework that translates global market structure and cross-asset capital flows into systematic portfolio decisions.

The core transmission chain is:

**Structure  
→ Capital Flow  
→ Risk Budget  
→ Volatility-Controlled Exposure  
→ Tactical Allocation  
→ ETF Execution**

It is designed not to predict markets, but to systematically interpret capital flow, control portfolio risk, and translate macro information into disciplined positioning.

> **Independent research project — not investment advice.**

---

# 🧠 Overview

Global Capital Flow Monitor is a rule-based macro strategy and validation framework designed to interpret **cross-asset capital-flow dynamics** and translate them into a structured daily strategist process.

The system integrates multiple layers of global market structure:

- Interest Rate Structure
- USD Conditions
- Volatility Regimes
- Credit Market Stress
- Liquidity Plumbing
- Policy Bias & Expectations
- Institutional Flow
- Positioning & Gamma Context
- Risk Budgeting
- Volatility-Controlled Exposure
- Sector Allocation
- Tactical Portfolio Construction
- ETF Execution Translation

The objective is not prediction.

The objective is:

- disciplined interpretation of market structure
- systematic risk budgeting
- tactical asset allocation
- execution translation
- auditable decision-making

The project has evolved from a daily macro monitoring engine into a **historical research and validation framework** designed to test whether portfolio decisions can be reproduced using the information and decision process that would actually have been available at the time.

# 🏦 Institutional Flow Architecture

The Institutional Flow Engine attempts to distinguish short-term price movement from more persistent capital repositioning.

Rather than treating price movement alone as evidence of institutional activity, the layer evaluates multiple forms of confirmation.

### Core Inputs

- multi-day drift
- cross-asset confirmation
- dealer gamma context
- positioning stress
- flow continuity
- participation behavior

### Flow Classification

- NO CLEAR FLOW
- EARLY TRACE
- FLOW BUILDING
- CONFIRMED FLOW

Conceptually:

```text
Price Movement
        ↓
Cross-Asset Confirmation
        ↓
Persistence
        ↓
Positioning / Gamma Context
        ↓
Flow Classification
```

### Strategic Use

Flow signals do not independently determine the macro regime.

They modify portfolio conviction within the broader market structure.

The central question is:

> **Is this simply price movement, or is there evidence consistent with persistent capital repositioning?**

- structural conditions
- institutional flow

The resulting Risk Budget becomes an upstream constraint for portfolio exposure.


# 🔄 Stateful Portfolio Logic

Not every portfolio decision is determined solely by today's signal.

The framework contains persistence and rebalance mechanisms designed to reduce unnecessary portfolio churn and preserve previously accepted portfolio states until sufficient evidence exists for transition.

Because this creates path dependence, historical validation explicitly audits:

- previous portfolio state
- persistence decisions
- state transitions
- execution consequences

# 🔬 Historical Research & Validation Framework

The project does not treat an attractive historical equity curve as sufficient evidence that a strategy is valid.

Before performance evaluation, the research process attempts to establish **decision-process integrity**.

## 1. Input Contract

Each strategy component is audited to determine exactly what information it consumes.

Examples include:

- rates
- USD
- volatility
- oil
- credit spreads
- liquidity
- positioning
- institutional flow
- market regime

---

## 2. Point-in-Time Integrity

Historical simulations should use information that would have been available at the relevant decision date.

Validation therefore considers:

- historical availability
- signal date vs. execution date
- lag structure
- previous-state dependencies
- historical data revisions where relevant

The objective is to reduce look-ahead bias and future-data leakage.

---

## 3. Production ↔ Backtest Parity

Research and production logic are intentionally separated while historical replay attempts, where possible, to reproduce the same decision process used by the production research engine.

```text
Historical PIT Inputs
        ↓
Production Decision Logic
        ↓
Backtest Replay
        ↓
Parity Audit
```

The purpose is to prevent the backtest from silently becoming a different strategy from the live research system.

---

## 4. Causal Attribution

When a model component changes, the research process traces where that change propagates.

For example:

```text
Macro Classification
        ↓
Portfolio Regime
        ↓
Risk Budget
        ↓
Exposure
        ↓
Sector Allocation
        ↓
Executed Equity Allocation
```

The objective is to distinguish explainable causal transmission from unexplained downstream differences.

---

## 5. Execution Contract Validation

Portfolio execution is independently audited against upstream capital constraints.

A historical execution audit identified cases where persisted sector weights could exceed a subsequently reduced exposure ceiling.

The reconciliation layer was introduced specifically at the execution boundary while preserving upstream strategy behavior.

Full-history validation confirmed that the capital constraint violation was removed without altering normal-date behavior.

---

## 6. Performance Comes Last

Returns, PnL, CAGR, Sharpe and other performance statistics are intentionally separated from model-selection stages when appropriate.

The governing principle is:

> **Understand why the system behaves differently before asking whether the difference made more money.**

This reduces the risk of selecting rules simply because they improve historical performance.

# 🎯 Strategic Differentiation

Global Capital Flow Monitor is not intended to be a collection of independent technical indicators.

Its primary distinction is the attempt to maintain an explicit transmission hierarchy from **market structure to portfolio execution**.

### 1. Macro-to-Execution Translation

```text
Macro
→ Flow
→ Risk Budget
→ Exposure
→ Allocation
→ ETF Execution
```

Each downstream decision remains constrained by upstream risk decisions.

### 2. Structural & Liquidity Plumbing

The framework incorporates financial-system liquidity conditions including TGA, RRP, Federal Reserve balance-sheet liquidity, credit spreads and cross-asset market behavior.

### 3. Risk & Allocation Hierarchy

Portfolio conviction is translated into explicit Risk Budget and Exposure layers before capital reaches sector allocation.

### 4. Stateful Decision Architecture

Persistence mechanisms reduce unnecessary portfolio churn while historical audits explicitly account for the path dependence they introduce.

### 5. Research Governance

Research hypotheses, production logic, historical validation and performance evaluation are deliberately separated.

Model changes are evaluated through input-contract, point-in-time, parity, causal-propagation and execution audits before performance is used as evidence.
